Research / Project

Exploratory research

Bio-derived functional materials

I explore whether recovered biomass components can serve as precursors for useful fiber-based materials.

Research question

Can lignin-containing formulations be processed into continuous fibers through electrospinning?

Approach

Initial formulation trials use aqueous polyvinyl alcohol and alkali lignin. I consider solution preparation, flow rate and process stability.

Current status

This is a feasibility investigation. Successful nanofiber formation and application performance have not yet been established.

Next steps

Evaluate formulation properties and spinning conditions, characterize fiber morphology, and assess material properties before proposing applications.
