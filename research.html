Research / Overview

From agricultural residues to functional materials

My research examines how feedstock composition, particle charging and process conditions influence dry biomass fractionation, with the longer-term goal of developing useful fiber-based materials.

Research themes

01 · Active

Selective biomass fractionation

Compare wheat bran and oat hull-derived fiber flour to understand component recovery and enrichment.

02 · Active

Particle charging & process physics

Investigate how contact materials, tube length and dosing influence charge-to-mass ratio and migration.

Explore project →

03 · Exploratory

Bio-derived functional materials

Explore electrospinning as a route to lignin-containing fibers and assess formulation feasibility.

Experimental workflow

Methods, datasets and tools

Experimental

Particle & process measurements

Faraday cup and electrometer; controlled airflow and dosing; electrode fraction collection; mass balances.

Analytical

Composition & characterization

Structural carbohydrate and lignin analysis, HPLC and UV–Vis; FTIR and SEM planned as complementary characterization.

Computational

Analysis & reproducibility

Excel, MATLAB, SPSS, Python and Polymath. I evaluate yield, enrichment, component recovery and experimental variability.

Research direction

I aim to establish experimentally defensible relationships between feedstock properties and separation behavior, then evaluate process optimization and downstream material performance. Energy and environmental benefits remain objectives to quantify, not established outcomes.
