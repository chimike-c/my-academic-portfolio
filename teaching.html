Teaching / Faculty readiness

Teaching for understanding, application and growth

I approach engineering education as a process of helping students develop conceptual understanding, technical confidence and the ability to apply knowledge independently.

Teaching philosophy

I believe students learn engineering most effectively when they understand why a concept matters, practice it deliberately and connect it to an authentic problem. I use clear explanations, guided practice and questions that make reasoning visible rather than rewarding memorization alone.

I aim to create an inclusive learning environment where students can ask questions, receive constructive feedback and develop confidence through progressively challenging tasks. I view assessment as a tool for learning: identifying misconceptions early, supporting revision and encouraging reflection.

As a future faculty member, I intend to integrate research-informed examples, laboratory inquiry and accessible course design, and to evaluate my teaching through student feedback and evidence of learning.
