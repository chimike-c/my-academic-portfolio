Research / Project

Active doctoral research

Selective biomass fractionation

I investigate whether biochemical composition influences tribo-electrostatic separation (TES) of agricultural residues.

Research question

How do protein-containing wheat bran and low-protein oat hull-derived fiber differ in particle collection and fiber-component recovery under comparable conditions?

Approach

I compare electrode and bottom fractions, quantify mass yield and component recovery, and evaluate enrichment relative to the original feedstock.

Preliminary interpretation

Observed differences in fraction distribution motivate a composition-dependent charging hypothesis. High component recovery does not necessarily imply high selectivity, and the existing results do not establish protein as the causal factor.

Next steps

Control dosing, airflow and voltage independently; measure charge under matched conditions; use complementary chemical and morphological characterization; optimize enrichment alongside recovery.
